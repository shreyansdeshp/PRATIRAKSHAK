## 7. Counterfactuals

- **Late detection** (sensor data arrives d days late and is treated as current): unplanned failures 1.2 → 2.3 (1 d) → 3.1 (3 d) → 8.8 (7 d); in-flight failures 1.0 → 8.0 (7 d). Availability over the 90 days: 68 aircraft-days gained at 3 d, 127 aircraft-days gained at 7 d. Acting later also means fewer, later hangar visits, and some deferred work falls beyond the 90-day horizon, which flatters availability; the failure counts are the honest cost of late detection.
- **2× usage:** under P3 hangar days 318 → 475; sensor faults 269 → 399; verify-sensor actions 9 → 16. Under P0 unplanned failures 181 → 275.
